Creamy White Sauce Pasta

A rich, velvety white sauce pasta made with butter, garlic, milk, and cheese. Creamy, comforting, and perfectly seasoned, it coats each bite of pasta in smooth, savory deliciousness.

INGREDIENT LIST

  • pasta
  • milk
  • cornflour
  • oil
  • water
  • cheese
  • salt to taste

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Bring salted water to a boil and add your choice of pasta. Cook until al dente, then drain and set aside. Reserve a cup of pasta water for later.
  2. In a pan over medium heat, melt butter and add finely chopped garlic. Sauté until fragrant and slightly golden, releasing the flavors into the butter for a rich aroma.
  3. Stir in a tablespoon of all-purpose flour to the butter-garlic mix. Keep stirring until it forms a smooth, golden paste. This thickens the sauce and adds a nutty base.
  4. Slowly pour in warm milk, whisking continuously to avoid lumps. Let it simmer on low heat, stirring until it thickens to a smooth, creamy consistency that coats the spoon.
  5. Add grated cheese—like mozzarella or parmesan—and stir until melted. Adjust seasoning with salt and black pepper. A pinch of chili flakes or herbs adds a flavorful twist.
  6. Toss the cooked pasta into the sauce, mixing until well-coated. Add reserved pasta water if needed to loosen. Serve hot, garnished with herbs or extra cheese for flair.
