PERI PERI FRIES

Delicious Peri Peri Fries

Peri peri fries are crispy, golden potato fries tossed in a zesty, spicy peri peri seasoning. Bursting with bold flavor, they’re the perfect fiery snack or side dish.

INGREDIENT LIST

  • large potatoes
  • oil
  • paprika
  • garlic powder
  • chili powder
  • salt

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Peel and cut potatoes into thin fries. Soak them in cold water for 20 minutes to remove starch.
  2. Drain and pat dry the fries completely using a kitchen towel or paper napkin.
  3. Preheat oven to 220°C (428°F) or set up air fryer. Toss fries with oil and all spices in a bowl.
  4. Spread fries on a baking tray or in air fryer basket in a single layer.
  5. Bake or air-fry for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway until golden and crisp.
  6. Serve hot with your favorite dip or drizzle with extra peri peri sauce for more spice
