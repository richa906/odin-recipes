OREO MUG CAKE

chocolatey mug cake

A quick and delicious Oreo mug cake made with crushed Oreos and milk, microwaved to perfection. Soft, chocolatey, and ready in minutes — the perfect no-fuss dessert for any craving!

INGREDIENT LIST

  • Oreo cookies
  • milk
  • baking powder
  • sugar
  • chocolate chips

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Place Oreos in a microwave-safe mug and crush them into fine crumbs using a spoon or fork.
  2. Pour in the milk and mix until it forms a smooth batter. Add baking powder and sugar if desired.
  3. Stir in chocolate chips or extra crushed Oreos for more texture.
  4. Microwave on high for about 1 minute. Check with a fork; if it’s still gooey, heat 10-15 seconds more.
  5. Let it sit for a minute to avoid burning your mouth.
  6. Eat straight from the mug! Top with whipped cream or ice cream if you like.
